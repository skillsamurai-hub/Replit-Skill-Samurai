Build a web app called "Skill Samurai Cancellation & Pause Request Form" using React with a clean, friendly design (Tailwind CSS). Single page, mobile-friendly.

FUNCTIONALITY:

1. Landing screen with two options as large buttons:
   - "Cancel Enrollment"
   - "Pause Enrollment"

2. If "Pause" is selected:
   - Show a simple form: Parent Name, Child Name, Email, Phone
   - Field: "How many months would you like to pause?" (dropdown: 1, 2, 3 months)
   - Show message: "Your child's spot will be held and billing will pause during this time. No further charges will occur until your pause period ends or you choose to restart sooner."
   - Submit button: "Request Pause"

3. If "Cancel" is selected:
   - Show form fields: Parent Name, Child Name, Email, Phone, Next Billing Date (date picker), Reason for Leaving (dropdown: Moving away, Too expensive, Schedule conflict, Child lost interest, Other)

   - Before showing the branch logic, display a neutral "Your Enrollment Record" recap box using this exact language to match the original enrollment form:
     "For reference, here's what's on file for your account:
      ✓ You initialed the Cancellation Policy during online enrollment on [enrollment date placeholder], which states: 'Monthly memberships require 30 days cancellation... we are not able to refund you for any classes that you have missed in the months before.'
      ✓ A reminder of this policy was also included in your welcome email on [welcome email date placeholder]
     This form will walk you through your next steps based on that policy."
     (Use a neutral gray/blue box, checkmark icons, non-alarming tone — this is informational, not accusatory)

   - Calculate automatically: days between today's date and the "Next Billing Date" entered

   - LOGIC BRANCH A (if days between today and Next Billing Date is 30 or more):
     - Show green confirmation box: "Good news — you're providing enough notice. Your enrollment will end on [Next Billing Date] with no additional charges."
     - Show button: "Confirm Cancellation"

   - LOGIC BRANCH B (if days between today and Next Billing Date is less than 30):
     - Show a notice box: "Your request is within 30 days of your next billing date. Per the 30-day notice policy initialed at enrollment (referenced above), please choose one of the following options:"
     - Show two selectable option cards:
       a) "Extend One More Month" — description: "Your child continues attending classes for one more billing cycle. No further charges after this final month."
       b) "Credit on File" — description: "The remaining value is held as a credit on your account for future use (re-enrollment or another program). No further charges will occur."
     - Require the parent to select one option before submitting
     - Show button: "Confirm Selection"

4. On any submission, show a confirmation screen:
   - "Your request has been received."
   - Summary of what was selected (cancel/pause, dates, chosen option)
   - Recap of the enrollment record (initialing date + welcome email date) shown again for the parent's records
   - Text: "A confirmation email has been sent to [email]. If you have any questions, contact us at [phone/email placeholder]."

5. Store all submissions in a simple backend (use Replit DB or a JSON file) with fields: timestamp, parent name, child name, email, phone, request type (cancel/pause), next billing date, days notice given, branch (A or B), option chosen (credit/extend/pause length), reason for leaving, enrollment initial date, welcome email sent date.

   - For now, use placeholder/mock values for "enrollment initial date" and "welcome email sent date" (e.g., a fixed date or a simple lookup by email in a mock JSON dataset), since real integration with Jumbula will come later.

6. Add a simple admin view at /admin (password protected, simple hardcoded password for now) that lists all submissions in a table, sortable by date, including the enrollment initial date and welcome email date columns.

DESIGN NOTES:
- Use calm, neutral colors (blues/greens), not red or alarming colors — this should feel like a routine process form, not a punitive one
- Avoid the word "fee" or "penalty" anywhere in the copy
- Keep language plain and reassuring throughout
- The "Enrollment Record" recap box should feel like a helpful reference, not a "gotcha" — informational tone, not confrontational
- Use a progress indicator showing steps (e.g., Step 1: Request Type, Step 2: Details, Step 3: Confirmation)

Do not integrate real email sending yet — just simulate the confirmation email with a placeholder message on screen for now.
