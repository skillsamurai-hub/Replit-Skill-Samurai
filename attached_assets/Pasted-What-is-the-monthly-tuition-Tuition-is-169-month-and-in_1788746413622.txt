What is the monthly tuition?

Tuition is $169/month and includes weekly coding classes, curriculum, software, laptops, and materials.

There is a one-time $99 registration fee.

Early Bird Offer: Register during Early Bird Enrollment and use promo code STA2026 to have the $99 registration fee waived.

Students continuing from Term 1 into Term 2 will also have the $99 registration fee waived.

Who can join the St. Alphonsus Coding Club?

Classes are designed for students in Grades 1–8.

3:15–4:15 PM — Reserved for St. Alphonsus students only
4:30–5:30 PM — Open to all students
5:45–6:45 PM — Open to all students

When are classes?

Classes are held on Fridays and are offered in two 4-month terms:

Term 1: October–January
Term 2: February–May

There are no classes on school closures, holidays, or scheduled non-instruction days. Families will receive a full class calendar for each term.

My child has never coded before. Will they fit in?

Absolutely! No previous coding experience is required.

Students work at their own level and pace with support from our Skill Samurai instructors.

What will my child learn?

Students learn real coding skills through fun, hands-on projects using Scratch, Roblox, game design, and more.

Along the way, they'll develop problem-solving, creativity, logical thinking, and confidence with technology.

Does my child need to bring a laptop?

No. Skill Samurai provides the laptops, software, and everything students need for class.

How does the 3:15 PM class work for St. Alphonsus students?

Students registered for the 3:15–4:15 PM class can transition directly from the school day into Coding Club.

We'll coordinate with the school and maintain a clear list for parent pickup or return to the school's after-school program after class.

What if my child misses a class?

Make-up classes are available at either of our two Winnipeg Skill Samurai locations, subject to availability.

Simply select Book a Make-Up Class on our website or call us at 431-998-2155.

How big are the classes?

Each class is limited to 20 students, with one Skill Samurai instructor for every 10 students.

Do we need to register again for Term 2?

Yes. Term 1 and Term 2 are registered separately.

Families who want to continue into Term 2 will need to re-register to reserve their child's spot. Spaces are first-come, first-served.

Continuing students will not pay the $99 registration fee again.

What is the cancellation policy?

Registration reserves your child's spot for the full 4-month term.

Once registration is confirmed, our instructors, curriculum, software, and program resources are planned and allocated based on enrollment for the full term.

For this reason, cancellations, pauses, and early withdrawals are not available once the term begins.

If your child misses a class, make-up classes are available at either of our two Winnipeg locations, subject to availability.
